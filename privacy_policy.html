Privacy Policy for Beautiful China

Introduction

Welcome to Beautiful China ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we collect, use, and share your information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and usage when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Data Collected Automatically

When you use our App, we may automatically collect certain information through third-party advertising services:

1. Advertising and Analytics Data

  • Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA): We collect your device's advertising identifier to deliver personalized advertisements and measure ad performance
  • Device Information: Device type, model, operating system version, screen resolution, and device identifiers
  • Network Information: IP address, network type (Wi-Fi, mobile data), and carrier information
  • App Usage Data: Information about how you interact with advertisements, including ad views, clicks, and impressions
  • Location Data: Approximate location data (derived from IP address) may be collected for ad targeting purposes

2. Technical Data

  • Crash Reports: Information about app crashes and errors to improve app stability
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics and usage statistics

Data Stored Locally (Not Transmitted)

The following data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or third parties:

  • Favorite Scenic Spots: IDs of scenic spots you mark as favorites
  • Favorite Wallpapers: IDs of wallpapers you mark as favorites
  • App Preferences: Local app settings and preferences

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  1. Advertising: To display relevant advertisements through our advertising partners
  2. Analytics: To understand how users interact with our App and improve user experience
  3. App Functionality: To provide and maintain our App's features and services
  4. Technical Support: To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance
  5. Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

Third-Party Services

Our App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

TradPlus SDK

We use TradPlus SDK (version 15.2.0.1) for advertising services. TradPlus and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Device identifiers (including Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information
  • Location data (approximate, derived from IP)
  • App usage data related to advertisements

TradPlus Privacy Policy: https://www.tradplus.com/privacy-policy

Advertising Partners

TradPlus SDK works with multiple advertising networks and partners. These partners may collect and use data according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more information.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  1. Advertising Partners: We share data with TradPlus and its advertising network partners to deliver and measure advertising effectiveness
  2. Service Providers: Third-party service providers who assist us in operating our App and conducting our business
  3. Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or governmental authority

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

  • Advertising Data: Retained by our advertising partners according to their respective retention policies
  • Local Data: Favorite lists and preferences stored locally on your device until you uninstall the App or clear app data

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Advertising ID Controls

  • Android: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:
    • Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: You can limit ad tracking through Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking

Local Data Controls

  • You can clear your favorites and app data at any time through your device's app settings
  • Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data

If you wish to access, correct, or delete your personal information, please contact us using the information provided in the "Contact Us" section below.

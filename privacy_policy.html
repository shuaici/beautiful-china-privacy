Privacy Policy for Beautiful China

Last Updated: April 3, 2026

Introduction

Beautiful China ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile application that helps you explore scenic spots and related content in China. This Privacy Policy explains what information is involved when you use the App and how it is handled.

We do not operate a user login system in the App. We do not require you to create an account to use core features. We do not sell your personal information.

By using the App, you acknowledge this policy.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs in this version of the App.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (such as GAID) for ads or ad personalization.
  • We do not upload your favorites, quiz scores, or other locally stored app data to our own servers (we do not provide a central account backend for this App).

Network Access and Third-Party Content

The App uses the Internet permission to load scenic descriptions, images, and optional supplemental data from remote locations configured in the App (for example, public repositories, image hosting, or content delivery networks used to serve pictures and text). When your device requests such content:

  • Your device sends standard web requests (typically over HTTPS).
  • Those servers may process technical metadata that is common for any website or API request, such as IP address, request headers, and timestamps, according to their own policies.
  • We do not control those third-party servers; please refer to their privacy notices if you need details.

Image and text loading is used solely to display tourism-related content inside the App.

Data Stored on Your Device Only

The following data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us as part of a user account (because the App does not use such an account):

  • Favorites: identifiers of scenic spots (or similar items) you mark as favorites.
  • Quiz and game results: scores or records from in-app quizzes, stored locally (e.g., in a local database).
  • Other preferences: app settings stored on device (e.g., via local storage).

This data remains on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall the App, or use in-app controls where available.

Permissions

The App requests the minimum permissions needed for its functionality. Typical permissions include:

  • Internet access: to load remote content described above.

If future versions add permissions (for example, for notifications), we will update this policy and the Play Store listing accordingly.

Security

We design the App to use secure connections where remote content is loaded (HTTPS is used when the remote endpoint supports it). No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security of third-party networks.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently processed such information, please contact us using the details below.

Your Choices

  • You can remove local data by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • You can disable network access for the App through device settings (note that content that requires network loading may not work).

International Users

If you use the App outside your country of residence, your device may connect to servers located in various regions when loading third-party content. By using the App, you understand that such connections may occur.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (summary)

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, applicable data protection laws may grant you rights such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or objection regarding personal data. Where our processing is limited to local-only data on your device that we do not receive on our servers, many of these rights are exercised directly on your device (e.g., clearing app data). For questions, contact us below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Google Play & Data Safety

For the Google Play Data Safety section, we declare data practices consistent with this policy: in the current version, we do not collect advertising IDs through the App for advertising, and local favorites and scores are stored on the device as described above. Please ensure the answers in Play Console match this policy at the time you submit or update the app.
